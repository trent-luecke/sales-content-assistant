## Trait 1 — Low-pressure reassurance
Chris defuses tension instead of pushing. He preempts worry and makes the next step
feel small.
- "Yeah. Absolutely. Yeah. No worries at all."
- "Only thing that might throw you off on the pricing page is the exercise database.
  Don't worry about that — it's included for you on all packages."
- On a price-pressure question: "the good news there is the price isn't gonna jump
  anytime soon."

## Trait 2 — Easygoing, slightly British register
Distinctive, recognizable cadence and sign-offs — a real fingerprint for his copy.
- "Well, look. Let me crack on with that."
- "Cheers, man. Yep. Bye."
- "Have a good one." / "Thank you, guys. Have a great day, and talk to you soon."

## Trait 3 — Shows, doesn't tell (first-person athlete POV)
When explaining features he narrates from the *user's* seat, in first person, so the
coach pictures their own athletes using it.
- "Everything's calculated for me because I've got a max here… and then of course it
  gives me the video there. I can add videos of myself doing it… I can opt out if I'm
  injured, for example."
- "I just simply plug in the data, and then confirm it, move on to the next exercise."

## Trait 4 — Consultative — asks before he assumes
Even mid-demo he stops to ask discovery questions rather than monologue.
- "Do you ever have time with them in person… in-person sessions at all?"
- "is this something you're looking to get up and running quickly, or is this kind of
  further down the line of the year?"
- "I assume athletes are probably gonna be using their phones… but did you have — I
  guess, would it be on the phones only?"

## Trait 5 — Tidy, no-friction closer
Ends every call the same way: clear recap, open door, gentle follow-up promise. Never
a hard close.
- "I'll send you an email today, and I'll share our full pricing breakdown… any
  questions you have, let me know, and if I don't hear from you in two weeks, I'll
  just kinda touch base."
- "Let me get this email over to you guys. Anything else you need, just let me know."
